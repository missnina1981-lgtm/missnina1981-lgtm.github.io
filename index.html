About the Ministry

Who we are, in our own words.

Our Purpose

At SOUL Wilderness Ministries, we seek to help broken and lost souls rediscover the joy of living by avenues for connection, healing, and growth within a supportive community. We believe that restoration is possible through genuine relationships and outreach initiatives that meet the physical, emotional, and spiritual needs of those we serve.

Our Mission

Our mission is to transform the lives of the individuals we work with by guiding them to a place of servitude. We encourage personal growth, selfless service, and the cultivation of leadership qualities that empower each person to contribute positively to their communities. We aim to provide practical skills and spiritual mentorship, fostering a sense of purpose grounded in love and service.

Our Vision

We envision a world where the dark spots of despair and loneliness are outnumbered by beacons of light-individuals empowered by love and support who shine brightly in their communities. Our goal is to create a ripple effect of hope and transformation, inspiring others to join us in our mission to illuminate the path towards healing, purpose, and connection.

Our Values

At Soul Wilderness we uphold values of Compassion, Community, and Service, believing that every soul has inherent worth and potential. We are committed to cultivating an atmosphere of love, respect, and inclusion, ensuring that all who enter our ministry feel welcomed and valued as they embark on their journey toward wholeness.
